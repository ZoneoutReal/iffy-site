Privacy Policy

App: Iffy: If-Then Cards
Publisher: Totem Works
Effective date: October 29, 2026

In short: everything you create in Iffy stays on your device. Iffy has no account, no tracking, no analytics, and no ads. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your data.

What Iffy stores

Iffy stores the cards you write (the situation, the steps, your color choice, whether a card is pinned, and an optional reminder time) and a setting that remembers you have seen the welcome screen. This information is saved only in the app’s storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to Totem Works or to anyone else.

What Iffy does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it does not include third-party code that does.
  • It does not connect to a server of ours. The app contains no networking code for your cards.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

Features that use your device

  • Reminders. If you turn on a daily reminder, Iffy asks iOS for permission to send notifications and schedules them locally on your device. Reminders are not sent from a server.
  • Export and import. If you export your cards, Iffy creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Once you save or share that file, how it is handled is up to you and the place you chose. Import reads only a file you pick.
  • Home Screen widget, Spotlight, and Shortcuts. To show pinned cards in the widget and to let you find or open cards from Spotlight and Shortcuts, Iffy shares card text with those parts of iOS on your device. This stays on your device and is handled by iOS.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup or to a computer), the backup may include Iffy’s data, depending on your device settings. That backup is handled by Apple and your settings, not by Totem Works.

Children

Iffy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete cards inside the app, or delete the app, to remove its data from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
