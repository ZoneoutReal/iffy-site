Common questions

How do I make a card?

On the Cards screen, tap the plus button. Write the situation after “If…”, then add up to three short “Then…” steps. Blank steps are left out when you save.

How do I use a card in the moment?

Tap the card in the list. The plan opens in large text.

How do I pin, reorder, edit, or delete a card?

  • Pin: swipe a card to the right in the list, or open the card and tap the pin button. Pinned cards stay at the top.
  • Reorder: tap Edit in the list, then drag a card by its handle. Clear the search box first.
  • Edit: open the card and tap the edit button.
  • Delete: swipe a card to the left in the list, then confirm.

Can I get the starter cards back, or remove them?

Yes. Open Settings from the Cards screen. “Re-add missing starters” restores any starter you deleted and keeps your edits to the ones you still have. “Delete all starter cards” removes every starter, including edited ones. Cards you created yourself are not touched.

How do reminders work?

In the card editor, turn on “Daily reminder” and pick a time. The first time, iOS asks whether Iffy may send notifications. If you said no, you can allow them in the iOS Settings app under Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and start after you save the card.

How do I back up my cards or move them to a new iPhone?

Open Settings, then Data, then Export cards. You choose where the backup file is saved or shared. On the other iPhone, open Settings, then Data, then Import cards, and pick the file. Import adds cards that are missing and keeps the cards already on the device. Iffy has no cloud account, so keep your backup file somewhere safe.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete your cards in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone. Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the app removes its data from the device. Backup files you exported are separate files, so delete those yourself if you no longer want them.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Iffy a medical or treatment tool?

No. Iffy is a personal note-and-plan tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything and does not replace support from a health professional.
