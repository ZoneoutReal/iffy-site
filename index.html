Iffy: If-Then Cards

Know what to do, right then.

Iffy keeps your own “if this happens, I do that” plans one tap away. Each situation is a simple card: the “If” on top, and one to three short “Then” steps underneath. Open the app, tap the situation, and read the plan in big, clear text.

  • No account and no sign-up
  • No ads, no subscription
  • No tracking and no analytics
  • Your cards stay on your iPhone

What you get

  • About ten editable starter cards to begin with
  • Search, pin, reorder, edit, and delete
  • Large text that follows your Dynamic Type setting, VoiceOver support, light and dark appearance
  • An optional daily reminder for any card, scheduled on your device
  • Export your cards to a file and import them back
