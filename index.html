Iffy: If-Then Cards

Know what to do, right then.

Iffy keeps your own “if this happens, I do that” plans one tap away. Each situation is a simple card: the “If” on top, and one to three short “Then” steps underneath. Open the app, tap the situation, and read the plan in big, clear text.

  • No account and no sign-up
  • No ads, no subscription. Free to use, with unlimited cards
  • No tracking and no analytics
  • Your cards stay on your iPhone

What you get, free

  • A warm paper-card design with rounded, easy-to-read type
  • Unlimited cards in six colors, with about ten editable starter cards to begin with
  • Check off each step; a small ring fills in when you finish (it stays still if you use Reduce Motion)
  • Search, pin, reorder, edit, and delete
  • A Home Screen widget (small and medium) for your pinned cards
  • Large text that follows your Dynamic Type setting, VoiceOver support, light and dark appearance
  • An optional daily reminder for any card, scheduled on your device
  • Export your cards to a file and import them back

Iffy Plus (optional)

Iffy is free to use. If you’d like more ways to make it yours, Iffy Plus is a single, one-time purchase. It is not a subscription.

  • Extra palettes: Color Block, Ticket Banner, Outline Accent, and Soft Wash
  • A New York font option
  • Six extra card colors: Aqua, Cobalt, Chartreuse, Terracotta, Graphite, and Fuchsia
  • Alternate app icons: Sky, Mint, Rose, and Coral
  • Large Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
What stays free: everything above under “What you get, free”, including unlimited cards, reminders, export and import, and the small and medium widgets. Nothing that was free before Iffy Plus has moved behind it.

Price: $4.99 in the US, paid once. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

Already bought it? On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open Settings in Iffy, tap Iffy Plus, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. You don’t need an Iffy account; there isn’t one.

Privacy is unchanged. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Iffy still has no account, no analytics, and no tracking, and your cards still stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
